## How it works in 3 steps

### 1. Wake your monsters
Ghosts, Mad Scientists, Pumpkins, Skeletons, Vampires, Werewolves, and
Witches are ready to cause trouble. Everyone gets a randomized deck of
monsters and the tricks they can pull.

### 2. Trick or treat
Send a monster to treat and grab candy from the bowl, or to trick and
mess with your opponents, including eating their candy. Either way, the
monster gets tired out: rotate the card 90 degrees counter-clockwise.

### 3. Eat your way to victory
Everyone starts with 10 candy. Collect 31 to win, or be the last player
standing. Run out of candy and you're out of the game.
