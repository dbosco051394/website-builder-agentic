# Monster Book

## One-line pitch
Eat their hearts out! A fast-paced, spooky card game where the victory
points are real candy, and you can eat your opponents' points.

## Game basics
- Players: 2-4
- Ages: 10+
- Play time: 45-90 mins
- Format: physical box game (not a trading card game). One complete game,
  replayable, with expansions planned that add new mechanics and monster themes.
- Candy: each player starts with 10 pieces of small candy (jelly beans,
  Pez, any small candy).

## How it plays
Each player gets a randomized deck of classic Halloween monsters and the
tricks they can perform. Monsters are used for one of three actions:

- **Treating:** the monster grabs candy from the shared bowl.
- **Tricking:** the monster plays a trick card, which can do all sorts of things, such as drawing cards, making more monsters, and giving your opponents a headache.
- **Fighting** the monster attacks another monster or player. Fighting a monster results in both monsters becoming damaged, dying when they become too hurt. Fighting a player lets you eat their candy (you physically take it and eat it).

Either action makes the monster "tired out": rotate the card 90 degrees
counter-clockwise. Monsters become "ready" at the start of your turn: rotate the card 90 degrees clockwise.

## Monsters
Ghosts, Mad Scientists, Pumpkins, Skeletons, Vampires, Werewolves, Witches.

## Winning
There are two ways to win:
1. **Candy victory:** collect 31 candy in your candy bowl.
2. **Last one standing:** if you run out of candy, you are eliminated.
   The last player left in the game wins.

Each player starts with 10 candy. The shared bowl is bottomless: it is
simply the place players physically grab candy from when treating, and it
never runs out. Candy only leaves the game when a monster eats it.

Monsters can eat opponents' candy, reducing their total while you physically
eat the candy. Eating is how you knock opponents out of the game.

The bowl never empties. The only endings are 31 candy or last player
standing.

## Signature hook
Monsters can eat opponents' candy, and eating can eliminate players
from the game entirely. Physically eating your opponents' victory points
is the most popular part with playtesters and should be a central part of
the marketing. The tagline "Eat their hearts out!" ties directly to it.

## Candy and components
- Candy is NOT included in the box. Players supply their own.
- The shared bowl is bottomless (not a tracked or limited supply).
  It is only a physical place to grab candy from.
- Cardboard candy tokens ARE included for players who prefer not to eat
  candy. (Don't eat the cardboard!)
- Retail plan: hobby and game shops are encouraged to bundle a small pack
  of candy with the game at a small discount.
- Recommended candy amount per game or per player: 20 candy per player.
- Allergy note: encourage players to choose candy that suits everyone at
  the table, or use the cardboard tokens included in the box.
